# Product

Quiet News gives every visitor the same calm, concise digest of the most
important developments from the completed previous New York calendar day.

## MVP

- Public responsive website
- English language
- United States general audience
- Zero to five stories per edition
- Normal publication before early-morning readers arrive
- Public, traversable daily archive
- No accounts, personalization, filters, subscriptions, advertising, or
  notifications

Each story shows a factual headline and concise body. Supporting sources remain
in the public edition metadata for validation and later product use but are not
displayed initially.

Refreshing never generates content. The homepage reads the shared current JSON
edition. Archive links read immutable dated JSON files. If current publication
has failed and the previous edition has expired, the website explains that
today's edition could not be published instead of showing stale news.

Archive navigation is a compact `Today` dropdown. It opens a calendar where
dates with published editions are selectable and all other dates are disabled.

The footer identifies web-opt.com as the creator and states that Quiet News is
informational rather than an official source.

## Editorial behavior

The publisher selects zero to five important, well-supported stories and avoids
filler, sensationalism, unsupported claims, opinion, and calls to action. It
summarizes a completed calendar day rather than trying to be a live news feed.
The previous edition is context for continuity, not a required comparison.

A complete zero-story edition is valid. Partial or invalid output is never
published.
