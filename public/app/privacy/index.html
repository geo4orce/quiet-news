Quiet News privacy policy

This policy explains how WEB-OPT LLC handles information when you visit quietnews.ai website or use the "Quiet News" iOS/Android app.

For privacy questions or requests, email contact@web-opt.com.

Connecting to Quiet News

Connections to Quiet News use HTTPS. Our hosting and content delivery providers receive your IP address and basic request information to deliver and protect the service. They may retain operational records.

Offline copies

The iOS/Android app saves copies of public summaries in its private storage so you can read them offline. It keeps up to 30 dated summaries and a copy of the latest one. Deleting the app removes the local copies.

Links to other sites

When you open a source link, the destination site handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

Support email and deletion requests

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and anything you choose to include. We use this information to respond and investigate the issue. We keep correspondence as needed to handle the request and meet legal obligations. You can contact us at the address above to request deletion of your correspondence.
