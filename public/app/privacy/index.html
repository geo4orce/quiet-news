Quiet News privacy policy

WEB-OPT LLC operates the Quiet News website and mobile app.

Reading Quiet News

WEB-OPT LLC does not retain reader access logs.

The app saves summaries on your device for offline reading. Deleting the app removes these local copies.

Links to other sites

When you follow a source link, the other site's privacy policy applies to your visit.

Email and deletion requests

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you send to handle your request. We keep correspondence as needed to handle the request or as required by law.

For privacy questions or to request deletion of your correspondence, email contact@web-opt.com.
