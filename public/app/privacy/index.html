Quiet News privacy policy

This policy covers the Quiet News Android app, published by WEB-OPT LLC. For privacy questions or requests, email geo@web-opt.com.

Reading Quiet News

The app has no account, ads, advertising identifiers or in-app analytics. It requests the latest summary, archive index and selected days from quietnews.ai over HTTPS. Our hosting and content delivery providers receive your IP address and basic request information, such as the requested file, to deliver and protect the service. They may retain operational records.

Offline copies

The app saves copies of public summaries in its private storage so you can read them offline. It keeps up to 30 dated summaries and a copy of the latest one. Android’s Clear storage control or uninstalling the app removes the local copies.

Links to other sites

Tapping a source opens it in your browser. The destination site handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

Support email and deletion requests

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and anything you choose to include. We use this information to respond and investigate the issue. We keep correspondence as needed to handle the request and meet legal obligations. You can contact us at the address above to request deletion of your correspondence.
