Quiet News support

Quiet News is published by WEB-OPT LLC. For app help, questions or corrections, email contact@web-opt.com.

Using the app

Open the app for the latest daily summary and pull down to refresh it. Open Archive to read a published day. When you’re offline, the archive shows days you’ve already opened and the app has saved.

Reporting a problem

For an app problem, include the Android version and what happened. For a correction, include the date, headline and any relevant source link.

Quiet News uses AI to summarize reporting from multiple sources. Each story names and links to its sources. Verify important details with the original reporting.
